# Themes

## Player

Change in **Settings → General → Theme**. Applies immediately.

| Theme | Look |
|---|---|
| **Amber** *(default)* | Flat warm panel, one gold accent. Artwork edge to edge on the left with a shelf beneath it, all controls visible on the right |
| **SonosESP** | Blurred album art fills the screen behind the player |
| **Immersive** | Full-bleed colour and a large animated lyric stage |

The blurred backdrop on the SonosESP theme can be switched off in
**Settings → Display**. Amber and Immersive paint their own, so the setting does
not apply to them.

::: tip Ambient was removed in v2.0.0
Amber covers what it was for — a flat panel with the lyrics off the artwork —
and does it to a drawn design. A device set to Ambient moves to Amber on update;
every other theme keeps its selection.
:::

## Clock and screensaver

The panel falls back to a clock when idle. Change the face in
**Settings → Clock → Theme**. Each one supports the optional photo background and
the weather overlay.

| Face | Look |
|---|---|
| **Amber** *(default)* | Hours over minutes with a seconds hairline, weather column, 6-hour rail, and the paused track in the corner |
| **Horizon** | Centred clock, weather summary, forecast as pills |
| **Orbit** | Clock beside a sun-path arc tracking real sunrise and sunset |
| **Monolith** | Hours over minutes, details column, forecast rail |
| **StandBy** | Oversized digits tinted from the current album art |

Touch the screen at any point to go back to the player.

## Queue and Rooms

Both open **over** the player rather than replacing it, so the transport stays
reachable while you use them:

- **Queue** — a drawer from the right edge. Tap a track to play it; the footer
  shuffles or clears. Clear asks once before it does anything, because it empties
  the queue for everyone on that speaker.
- **Rooms** — a centred card listing every speaker found, with the selected one's
  volume inline.

Tap outside either one to dismiss it.
